**Paladins: Comp Analytics Tool** — advanced statistics, or just meow.

PaladinsCat is a community analytics platform that brings Paladins player profiles, match histories, champion statistics, and account signals together in one place. It also publishes technical investigations into game-data quality and match recovery.
